Slide 2 — Meet the Shark

What Is a Shark?

  • ▸Sharks are fish — but very special ones. They have been on Earth for over 450 million years!
  • ▸That means sharks are older than dinosaurs.
  • ▸Their skeletons are made of cartilage, not bone — the same material as your nose and ears.
  • ▸There are over 500 different species of sharks in the ocean.

Slide 3 — Super Powers

Their Amazing Bodies

  • ⚡Sharks can smell a single drop of blood from over 1 km away!
  • ⚡They have a special sense called the "sixth sense" — they can detect electricity from other animals.
  • ⚡Sharks grow thousands of teeth in their lifetime — new ones grow in when old ones fall out.
  • ⚡Their skin feels like sandpaper and is covered in tiny tooth-like scales.

Slide 4 — Different Sharks

Types of Sharks

  • 🦈Great White Shark — the most famous. Can grow to 6 metres long!
  • 🦈Whale Shark — the biggest fish on Earth. It eats only tiny plankton.
  • 🦈Hammerhead Shark — has a very wide, flat head shaped like a hammer.
  • 🦈Dwarf Lanternshark — the smallest shark, only 20 cm long — fits in your hand!
500+ species worldwide 🌊

Slide 5 — Feeding

What Do Sharks Eat?

🐟
Fish & Squid

Most sharks eat fish, squid, and other sea creatures.

🦭
Seals & Sea Lions

Great whites hunt seals near coastlines.

🔬
Tiny Plankton

The biggest sharks — whale & basking — eat only microscopic plankton!

🦀
Crabs & Shellfish

Bottom-dwelling sharks eat crabs on the seafloor.

Slide 6 — The Truth

Are Sharks Dangerous?

  • ▸Sharks rarely attack humans. You are more likely to be struck by lightning!
  • ▸Most attacks happen because a shark mistakes a swimmer for a seal.
  • ▸Humans are actually far more dangerous to sharks — we kill around 100 million sharks every year.
  • ★Many shark species are now endangered and need our protection.

Slide 7 — Amazing Stories

Cool Shark Stories

  • 🌟Deep Blue is the largest great white shark ever filmed — she is about 6 metres long and believed to be over 50 years old!
  • 🌟A shark called "Lydia" was the first great white tracked swimming all the way across the Atlantic Ocean.
  • 🌟Sharks never get cancer — scientists are studying them to help find cures for humans.
  • 🌟Some sharks glow in the dark in the deep ocean using a special chemical in their skin!

Slide 8 — Why They Matter

Sharks & the Ocean

  • 🌊Sharks are apex predators — they sit at the very top of the food chain.
  • 🌊Without sharks, fish populations would explode out of control and damage coral reefs.
  • 🌊Healthy oceans need healthy shark populations — they keep the whole ecosystem balanced.
  • 🌊Protecting sharks means protecting our oceans — and our planet.

"Sharks are beautiful animals, and if you're lucky enough to see lots of them, that means the ecosystem is healthy."

— Dr Sylvia Earle, Ocean Explorer
